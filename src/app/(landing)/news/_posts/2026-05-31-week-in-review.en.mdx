# A Week in Covenant: Backdated Journals, a Real Profile Page, and a Combat Preview

Less of a single big shift this week, more a handful of features that each had a sharp edge I'd been ignoring for too long.

## The journal remembers

I shipped the journal module assuming users would write in it the same day something happened. But then I noticed I frequently write in my journal the next morning. So I improved it:

- **Backdate entries.** Pick a past day and write into it
- **Infinite scroll** instead of a wall of every entry you've ever written loading at once
- **A per-day mana cap.** Journaling earns mana, and without a cap "write 40 one-word entries" becomes an infinite mana printer. Now there's a daily ceiling

## A fresh profile page

Account settings used to be scattered and half-missing. There's now a **dedicated profile page** that does the boring-but-essential things:

- Change your email and update your password
- A **danger zone** for deleting your account — walled off and deliberately striking

## The landing page throws a punch

The public page was all words.

- An **animated combat preview** in the hero banner that shows the game loop — completing tasks, earning mana, using an ability and killing demons
- Updated **coming-soon section** with factions, warfronts, calendar sync

## Small things with teeth

- **You're allowed to quit.** Quests got an abandon action with a confirm dialog, for cowards that don't have the balls to die in battle.
- **The calendar shows your mood.** Each day tints by how you logged it, so a glance at the month tells you something before you read a word

## Under the hood

- **Analytics, finally.** Wired up PostHog for events and feature flags, including an activation event so I can actually see whether people complete the core loop instead of guessing. Measure first, then decide what to cut
- **Migrated all IDs from UUIDs to bigints with separate public IDs.** Faster lookups, smaller indexes, and URLs stop leaking internal row counts. Pure plumbing, but the kind you only get to redo cheaply while the data model is still small
- **Fixed Google sign-up flow** — Google users were sneaking in the side door and skipping onboarding. Now they get routed through onboarding like everyone else. No special treatment
