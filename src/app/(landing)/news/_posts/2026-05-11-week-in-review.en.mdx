# A Week in Covenant: Guilds, Quests, and Better Sign-In

This has been the week I've worked the most on Covenant. Here's what's new.

## Sign in like a normal app

I used to send magic links for user authentication. It felt clever, but it was annoying. Digging through your inbox or waiting for the email to arrive was a pain in the ass. So I've changed authentication to a simple email and password now. You stay signed in for a month instead of a week, so you won't get logged out mid-streak. Forgot your password? Reset link is one click away.

## Guilds are here

Guilds are small groups of players who share a chat room, see each other's progress, and (soon) tackle goals together.

Grinding alone is just spreadsheet work with extra steps. It doesn't have to be like that.

- Create a guild, invite your friends with a link, or join one
- Roles for owners, officers, and members
- A chat room to share anything you want with your guild
- Member list with avatars, levels, and who's been active

Invite links can have an expiry date or a use limit. Click one while logged out? You preview the guild, sign up, and land right back in.

## Quests: a real adventure layer

Covenant used to have a public activity system where players contributed to an objective. But for the people who want a solo experience I've created a quest system with a simple combat system, inspired by Pokémon (please don't sue me (⊙＿⊙')).

## Daily journaling

Journaling has helped me a lot to reflect and think about my life. It has been kind of therapeutic. So I wanted to create an approachable experience in Covenant, where you can write anything about the day and describe your mood.

- A clean markdown editor for your journal entry.
- Pick your mood (associated with a color) — it feeds a calendar heatmap so you can see your week at a glance
- Creating an entry rewards you with mana to keep fighting.

Write about your day → get mana → destroy a demon. The pipeline of mental health.

## Better first thirty seconds ( • ᴗ - ) ✧

It's kind of overwhelming to enter a new app with many different options. For a first approach, I've set a tutorial to explain the basic mechanics of Covenant.

- A tutorial walks new players through tasks, habits, objectives, and quests.
- Every empty list now tells you what it's for and what to do next

## Emails actually arrive now

Covenant switched email providers so signup links actually reach your inbox. Emails now render in English or Spanish.
