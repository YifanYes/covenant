# News

Stay updated with the latest developments in the war against the Six Infernal Powers.

## Latest Updates

### September 2026 - Open Beta Launch

Covenant will be available in open beta. Join the Sacred Knights in the defense of Santa Cruz and begin your legend.

All closed beta users will retain their progress, items, and characters, plus receive secret and exclusive items and titles as a reward for their early support.

- **Character Creation**: Choose your class (Templar or Herald) and discover your magical nature
- **Productivity System**: Tasks, habits, and objectives now grant dice for combat
- **Combat System**: Reactive dice-based combat against the forces of evil
- **Quest System**: Take on combat missions from the quest board — patrol the gates, assault enemy strongholds, defend the walls — for gold and equipment
