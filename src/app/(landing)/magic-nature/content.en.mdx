# **Magic Nature**

Magic in Covenant is divided by the **nature** through which the user interacts with the elements of reality.

Everything that exists is composed of fundamental elements that can be shaped under two opposing philosophies: **Form** and **Void**.

> **Biblical reference:** Genesis 1:2 — "the earth was formless and void". The Void is the primordial state of pure potential; Form imposes structure upon it.

## **The Elements**

Elements are the building blocks of creation. Though neutral in essence, their manifestation changes drastically depending on the nature of who channels them.

- **Common Elements:** Fire, Air, Lightning, Earth, Water, Ice, Shadow, and Metal.
- **Unique Elements:**
  - **Light:** Exclusive to **Form**. Represents truth, structure, and revelation.
  - **Darkness:** Exclusive to **Void**. Represents the abyss, entropy, and the unknown.

---

## ⚖️ **Form**

Form gives structure to the shapeless. It imposes patterns, creates boundaries, defines reality. It is the magic of civilization, protection, and justice.

- **Philosophy:** Form takes what lacks definition and grants it purpose. Where there is molecular chaos, Form imposes cellular structure. Where there is dispersed energy, Form concentrates it at a point. For Form, magic is a tool to perfect the world and keep it safe from degradation.
- **Style:** Disciplined and precise. Form doctrines usually manifest with perfect geometric shapes, bright runes, and white or golden light. Predictable but extremely solid.
- **Typical Doctrines:**
  - **Oxygenation (Healing):** Structures blood flow, optimizes cellular respiration, imposes an ordered regeneration pattern on damaged tissues.
  - **Crystal Prison (Earth/Ice):** Traps the enemy in rigid molecular structures.
  - **Directed Lightning (Lightning):** Vibrates air particles to create an electrical potential with a precise trajectory toward the target.
  - **Condensed Fireball (Fire):** Creates a perfect sphere of fire from the carbon and oxygen in the air, with defined shape and controlled radius.
- **Attack (Offensive):** Based on **Judgment** and **Restriction**. Attacks through channeling pure energy or compact elements to "correct" the enemy's existence. Its attacks are direct and seek maximum efficiency.
- **Weaknesses:** It is rigid. If the enemy breaks the rules of the battlefield, Form users struggle to adapt. Requires total concentration and a mental state of calm and discipline.
- **Disadvantages and limitations:** Cannot return things to pure potential; can only maintain or restore what already exists. Overloading Form magic can turn the user into a dogmatic being, losing the ability to feel emotions or empathy in favor of pure logic.

---

## 🔥 **Void**

Void returns things to pure potential. It dissolves structures, transforms matter into energy, releases what was contained. It is the magic of untamed nature, passion, and entropy.

- **Philosophy:** The Void is the primordial state of the universe, prior to all form. Its users do not destroy: they _liberate_. They dissolve the bonds that hold matter together, returning it to its state of maximum potential. For the Void, structures are temporary prisons of energy.
- **Style:** Erratic, explosive, and devastating. Void doctrines manifest as dark flames, black lightning, fractures in space, and liquid shadows. Visually savage and difficult to control.
- **Typical Doctrines:**
  - **Sacrifice (Healing):** Restores life by draining it from enemies, allies, or the environment itself. Healing is fast but violent and often leaves scars.
  - **Internal Combustion (Fire):** Uses the oxygen in the enemy's body to burn them from within, releasing the energy stored in their cells.
  - **Dissolution (Lightning):** Releases the electrochemical bonds of the enemy's body, dissolving their molecular structure.
  - **Burning Tree (Fire):** Transforms a tree into pure flames, releasing all the solar energy stored in its matter over decades.
- **Attack (Offensive):** Based on **Liberation** and **Overload**. Its attacks seek to overwhelm defenses by releasing energy massively and unpredictably. Massive, destructive, and does not distinguish friends from foes if not careful.
- **Weaknesses:** It is self-destructive. If the user loses control, the magic dissolves their own body. Unstable and unreliable in long-duration combat if sacrifice is not constant.
- **Disadvantages and limitations:** Incapable of building or protecting durably. Prolonged use corrodes body and mind, leading the user toward madness or physical disintegration under the weight of their own power.

## **How Magic Works**

**How is it invoked?**
It is invoked through attunement with the soul's own nature. An individual is born with affinity to **Form** or **Void**.

- Form users channel mana through discipline, mantra, and iron will.
- Void users channel mana through intense emotion, instinct, and sacrifice of vital energy.

**What is its origin?**
It arises from the fundamental duality of the universe. Before the Big Bang, everything was Void: pure potential without form. The primordial explosion was the first act of Form, imposing structure on chaos. Since then, Form and Void battle for dominance: Form creates stars and life; Void consumes them and returns them to potential. Mana is the fluid that connects both poles.

**What does it look like?**

- **Form:** White, golden, or bluish glows. Geometric shapes, defined glyphs, and harmonic sounds (like a choir or bells).
- **Void:** Dark colors, reds, violets, or blacks. Fractal shapes, smoke, shadows, and discordant sounds (like rumbles or whispers).

**Difference between Magic and Matter**
The mechanical distinction is maintained:

- **Matter (Physical):** Exists on its own (a stone, a natural fire). Obeys common physical laws.
- **Magic (Mana):** Is an energy structure that imitates an element.
  - **Form Fire** imposes structure: creates a controlled flame with precise temperature, size, and direction.
  - **Void Fire** releases potential: dissolves the bonds of matter, transforming it into pure, uncontrollable energy.

**Protections** work the same: magical armor interrupts the mana structure (whether Form or Void), while physical armor stops mass.

**What makes magic easier?**
Acting according to your nature. A Knight of Form protecting their group channels their power fluidly. A Herald of Void releasing energy without restrictions unleashes immense power.

**What makes it harder?**
Attempting to use the opposite nature is impossible; the soul rejects the contrary frequency. What is difficult is controlling intensity: Form can become inert (powerless) and Void can become uncontrollable (dissolving the user).

**Are there dangers in using it?**

- **Form → Mental Petrification:** The user loses the capacity for change, becoming a living statue of rules and logic, unable to react to the unexpected.
- **Void → Entropic Corruption:** The body falls apart. Flesh becomes shadow or soot, and the mind is lost in a frenzy of meaningless liberation.

## **Arcane Mechanics**

Magic is neither infinite nor free. Every act of will that alters reality has a price.

### **1. Cost (Mana)**

Mana is the available mental and spiritual energy.

- **Recovery:** Mana regenerates slowly (except for specific doctrines). It can also be recovered with potions.
- **Depletion:** Reaching 0 mana causes "Magical Fatigue" (total Silence and -50% movement for 1 turn).

### **2. Risk (Nature Overload)**

Using magic intensively accumulates "Residue" in the soul. If the user's tolerance limit is exceeded, a negative effect activates according to affinity.

| Overload Level                  | Form Effect (Stasis)                                                  | Void Effect (Backlash)                                          |
| :------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mild** (3 doctrines/turn)     | -10% Movement Speed.                                                  | 5% damage to own health.                                        |
| **Moderate** (6 doctrines/turn) | **Rigidity:** You cannot dodge (Evasion = 0).                         | **Magical Bleeding:** Lose 5% health per turn.                  |
| **Critical** (Ultimate use)     | **Crystallization:** Stunned for 1 turn after finishing the doctrine. | **Collapse:** Receive double damage from any source for 1 turn. |

### **3. Mitigation (Foci)**

Magical equipment (Staves, Books, Talismans) acts as a heat sink for the soul.

- A good focus allows casting more doctrines before suffering Overload.
- Casting magic without a focus (bare hands) costs +50% mana and generates immediate Overload.

**Are people born with it?**
Yes. Every living being is born with an aligned "spark". An individual of Form will never be able to cast a Void doctrine, and vice versa. This affinity is absolute and defines the user's path in the world.
