# Roadmap

The battle for Santa Cruz is just the beginning. Here's what lies ahead for Covenant.

## Current Phase: Open Beta

The Silver Seat is under siege. Join the Sacred Knights and prove your worth.

### Available Now

- Character creation with Templar and Herald classes
- Magic nature discovery system
- Full task management (List, Calendar, Table, Matrix views)
- Habit tracking with streaks and bonuses
- Objectives and life areas
- Dice-based reactive combat
- Global activities and world events
- Equipment system with tiered gear
- Doctrine abilities for both classes
- Shop and inventory management

---

## Phase 2: The Expanding War

### Journaling System

- Daily reflection connected to your progress
- Document victories and analyze defeats
- Mood tracking tied to productivity

### PvP Duels

- Challenge other players using the dice system
- Your real consistency against theirs
- Ranked competitive seasons

### Leaderboards

- Global rankings for the most disciplined
- Weekly and monthly competitions
- Area-specific achievements

---

## Phase 3: New Horizons

### New Classes

- Expand your options with additional character classes
- Unique doctrines and playstyles
- Class-specific equipment

### Factions

- Join powerful factions with unique benefits
- Faction-wide events and competitions
- Exclusive faction equipment and doctrines

### Crafting and Cooking

- Create consumables from gathered materials
- Forge equipment with unique properties
- Recipe discovery system

---

## Phase 4: Beyond the Digital

### Board Game Release

- The physical version of Covenant arrives at your table
- Tactical combat with miniatures
- Solo and cooperative modes
- Campaign system with persistent progression
