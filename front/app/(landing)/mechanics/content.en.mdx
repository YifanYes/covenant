# Game Mechanics

ARQ combines productivity management with RPG progression. Every action you take in real life affects your character and the world.

## Productivity System

### Tasks

Tasks are the core of your productivity. Organize them using multiple views:

- **List View**: Simple, focused task management
- **Calendar View**: See your tasks across time
- **Table View**: Detailed view with sorting and filtering
- **Eisenhower Matrix**: Prioritize by impact and effort

High-impact tasks grant more dice when completed.

### Habits

Build consistency with the habit system:

- Track daily, weekly, or monthly habits
- Earn dice for each habit completion
- Build streaks for bonus rewards (21-day streaks grant extra dice)

### Objectives

Long-term goals that give direction to your tasks and habits:

- Link tasks and habits to objectives
- Complete objectives for major dice rewards
- Track progress across life areas

## Character System

### Classes

Choose your path at character creation:

- **Templar**: Warriors focused on physical strength and defense
- **Herald**: Mages who channel magical power through doctrines

### Stats

Your character has four core stats:

- **Health**: Your life force in combat
- **Mana**: Resource for casting doctrines
- **Strength**: Physical attack and defense power
- **Magic**: Magical attack and defense power

### Doctrines

Special abilities unique to your class:

- Each doctrine costs mana to use
- Equip up to 2 doctrines at a time
- Unlock more powerful doctrines as you progress

---

## Combat System

### Dice Mechanics

Combat uses a dice-based system:

1. **Earn Dice**: Complete tasks, habits, and objectives
2. **Roll Attacks**: Dice rolls determine your damage
3. **Roll Defense**: Block incoming enemy attacks
4. **Critical Hits**: Rolling 6s deals extra damage

### Activities

Global events where all players contribute:

- Defend Santa Cruz from demon invasions
- Earn gold and equipment as rewards
- Shape the narrative through collective success or failure

### Equipment

Gear up your character:

- **Weapons**: Increase attack dice and damage
- **Armor**: Improve defense and add bonus effects
- **Accessories**: Special abilities and stat bonuses
- **Tiers**: Higher tier equipment requires character progression
